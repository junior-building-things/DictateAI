Push-to-talk

Hold. Speak. Done.

Press your global hotkey from any app and start talking. A rolling pre-roll buffer means the start of your sentence is never clipped. Release, and the text lands right where your cursor is.

  • Global hotkey works everywhere — default ⌃⇧Space or Right Option
  • Hold-to-talk or toggle mode
  • Live overlay shows partial transcripts as you speak

Rewrite, not rephrase

Cleanup that respects your words.

A small language model removes filler, false starts, and stray “um”s, then adds punctuation — without paraphrasing or editorializing. What you meant, written cleanly.

  • Strips fillers, repeats, and self-corrections
  • Adds punctuation and casing
  • Never wraps your text in quotes or commentary
